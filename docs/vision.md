# UIN Vision

People do not need another social network.

They do not need more content, more scrolling or more digital noise.

They need to live more intentionally.

UIN exists to help people turn Intent into real-world Activity.

A person may have a short-term Intent for tonight.

A strategic Intent for the next year.

Or a Telos Intent that gives direction to an entire life.

UIN brings these Intentions into one living timeline.

Past Intent shows what a person has experienced.

Active Intent shows what they are doing now.

Future Intent shows where they are going.

This creates inspiration without attention addiction.

UIN is not a profile network.

UIN is not a content network.

UIN is not a dating app.

UIN is an Intent Network.

Everything starts with an Intent.
