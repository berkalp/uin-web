Build the world's first Intent Network.

Not by adding features.

By building the right foundations.

Every phase must strengthen the Intent ecosystem.

Never the attention economy.

# Success Metrics

The platform is successful when

People complete more Activities.

People discover new experiences.

People build meaningful relationships.

People return because life improved.

Not because scrolling became addictive.

---

# Things UIN Will Never Become

A social media platform.

A content platform.

A follower economy.

A short-video platform.

A popularity contest.

An attention marketplace.

These are permanent product boundaries.

---

# Long-Term Goal

Create a global network where people discover meaningful experiences through shared Intent.

Help people spend less time consuming content.

Help people spend more time intentionally living.

---

# Final Milestone

The vision is achieved when someone can honestly say:

"I have lived a richer life because UIN helped me turn my intentions into reality."
