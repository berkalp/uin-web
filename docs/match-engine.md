# Match Engine

Version 1.0

---

# Philosophy

The Match Engine is the heart of UIN.

It does not match people.

It matches Intent.

People become visible to each other only because an Intent creates a legitimate reason for connection.

Without Intent, there is no Match.

---

# Core Principle

Traditional platforms ask:

"Who should you meet?"

UIN asks:

"What are you trying to do?"

The answer creates the Match.

Intent always comes first.

---

# Matching Dimensions

A Match Score is calculated from multiple dimensions.

## Intent Similarity

Most important factor.

Examples

- Walking
- Coffee
- Theatre
- Cycling
- Learning Spanish

Weight:
Highest

---

## Intent Type

Intent types should generally match.

Examples

Tactical ↔ Tactical

Strategic ↔ Strategic

Telos ↔ Telos

Different types may still match when appropriate.

---

## Time Compatibility

Examples

Tonight

Tomorrow

Next week

Flexible

Recurring

The closer the schedules,
the higher the score.

---

## Geographic Compatibility

Distance between users.

Examples

Same venue

Same neighborhood

Same city

Nearby cities

Distance tolerance depends on Intent.

---

## Privacy Rules

Before any Match is shown:

Visibility permissions are evaluated.

Example

Friends Only

Close Friends

Invite Only

Selected Users

Excluded Users

Private

If privacy fails,

Match Score becomes zero.

---

## Capacity

Intent capacity affects matching.

Examples

Coffee

Capacity: 1

Football

Capacity: 22

Workshop

Capacity: 40

Book Club

Capacity: Unlimited

---

## Trust Score

Higher Trust Score increases priority.

Signals include

Completed activities

Identity verification

Reliable attendance

Positive reputation

---

## Reputation

Category-specific reputation matters.

Example

Someone may have

Excellent Hiking Reputation

Average Business Networking Reputation

Low Football Reliability

Each category has independent reputation.

---

## Friendship

Friendship never creates a Match.

It only increases the priority of an already compatible Match.

Intent remains the primary factor.

---

## Previous Activities

Users who successfully completed activities together receive a small compatibility boost.

This bonus must never dominate Intent similarity.

---

## AI Semantic Similarity

The AI understands that different wording may describe the same Intent.

Examples

"I want to drink coffee."

"Anyone for espresso?"

"Looking for a café."

All represent similar Intent.

---

# Match Score

Conceptually

```text
Match Score =
Intent Similarity
+
Time Compatibility
+
Location Compatibility
+
Trust Score
+
Reputation
+
Previous Successful Activities
+
Friendship Bonus
-
Risk Penalties
```

The exact weights may evolve over time.

---

# Risk Penalties

The Match Engine decreases score when detecting risks.

Examples

Repeated no-show

Spam behavior

Mass invitation

Rapid Intent creation

Harassment reports

Fake identity

Blocked users

Privacy conflicts

---

# Hard Filters

Some rules immediately reject matching.

Examples

Blocked user

Visibility restriction

Different age restrictions

Full capacity

Expired Intent

Cancelled Intent

Private Intent

Deleted profile

Hard Filters execute before Match Score calculation.

---

# Discovery Rules

Not people.

Discovery screens must primarily display

Time

Location

Category

Required participants

Only then

Organizer information.
