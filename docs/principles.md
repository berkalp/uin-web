# UIN Principles

## 1. Intent First

Everything starts with an Intent.

UIN is not built around profiles, content, followers or feeds.

UIN is built around Intent.

---

## 2. Everything is an Intent

Tactical, Strategic and Telos are not separate products.

They are different types of Intent.

Only the time horizon, complexity and completion logic change.

---

## 3. People become visible through Intent

UIN does not primarily help users discover people.

UIN helps users discover Intent.

People become relevant when their Intent intersects.

---

## 4. Profile is Intent Biography

A UIN profile is not a social media profile.

It is a living biography of a person's past, active and future Intent.

Profiles exist to inspire, not to entertain.

---

## 5. Friendship is a trust and visibility layer

Friendship exists to support Intent.

It controls visibility, invitation, trust and repeat collaboration.

Friendship is not the core product.

---

## 6. Activity validates Intent

An Intent becomes real when it turns into Activity.

Completed Activities build Reputation and enrich the Intent Portfolio.

---

## 7. Connection is an outcome

Connection is never the product.

Connection is the natural result of shared Intent and completed Activity.

---

## 8. Privacy belongs to each Intent

Profile visibility does not imply Intent visibility.

Every Intent has its own visibility rules.

Users decide who can see, join, follow or be excluded from each Intent.

---

## 9. No attention economy

No endless feed.

No reels.

No stories.

No likes.

No follower economy.

UIN measures success by completed Intent and real-world Activity, not screen time.

---

## 10. The core question

Every feature must answer one question:

How does this help users create, discover, match, follow or fulfill an Intent?
