# Intent Builder

Version 1.0

---

# Philosophy

The Intent Builder is the most important component of UIN.

Everything begins with an Intent.

Users never come to UIN looking for people.

They come because they want to do something.

The Builder exists to transform a human intention into a structured, understandable and matchable Intent.

Without Intent, nothing exists.

No Match.

No Activity.

No Experience.

---

# Core Principle

Users should never write long descriptions.

Creating an Intent should feel easier than sending a message.

Selection is always preferred over typing.

---
