# UIN Domain Model

## Core Philosophy

Everything in UIN begins with an Intent.

Intent is not a feature.

Intent is the central domain object of the entire platform.

Users do not come to UIN to meet people.

Users come to fulfill an Intent.

People become relevant only when their Intent intersects.

## Intent Types

Every Intent belongs to exactly one type.

### Tactical Intent

Short-term.

Usually completed within hours or days.

Examples

- Coffee tonight
- Saturday hiking
- Museum visit
- Concert

---

### Strategic Intent

Medium-term.

Requires planning and multiple Activities.

Examples

- Learn Spanish
- Build a startup
- Run a marathon
- Publish a book

---

### Telos Intent

Long-term.

Represents direction rather than completion.

Examples

- Help educate children
- Explore the polar regions
- Live sustainably
- Become a scientist

Telos Intent may never be "completed".

Its purpose is to provide direction.

## Intent Portfolio

Every person owns one Intent Portfolio.

Intent Portfolio consists of

- Past Intent
- Active Intent
- Planned Intent

Intent Portfolio represents how a person lives.

Not what they post.
